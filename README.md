## 📌 Project Overview

The **Global Superstore Sales Analysis** project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to understand overall business performance, profitability, delivery efficiency, and customer purchasing behavior.

Using Microsoft Excel, this project explores sales trends, product category performance, regional distribution, top-selling products, and customer segments to generate meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Evaluate sales across different regions and states.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Understand customer segment contributions.
- Evaluate average delivery time and shipping performance.
